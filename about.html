Joelle Yoon, B.S. Mechanical Engineering

Hi! My name's Joelle, and I'm a junior at Harvard studying Mechanical Engineering. I'm passionate about the intersection between Mechanical Engineering and Robotics, as well as Mechanical Design and Product Development. Recently, I've been especially interested in designing compact electromechanical systems, as well as exploring the diverse manufacturing processes that make up everyday objects in our lives.
